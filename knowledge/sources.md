# Knowledge base

27 short entries in two files:

- `emergency_guidance.json`: first-aid steps for 13 emergency situations
- `health_education.json`: 14 education topics, including WHO IMCI danger signs for children

Each entry has an `id`, a `topic`, its text, and a `source` field naming where the
guidance comes from (WHO fact sheets and guidelines, IFRC first-aid guidelines,
India's National Tuberculosis Elimination Programme, and Tele-MANAS). Entries marked
"Original demo content" were in the first version of the project.

The entries are paraphrased and shortened. They have not been reviewed by a clinician
and must not be used for real patients as they are. Before any real use, replace this
with a governed, versioned knowledge base checked against the current versions of the
source documents and local clinical protocols.
